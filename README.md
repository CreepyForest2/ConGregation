Medium-sized modpack with most recipes gregified
## About
ConGregation is a medium sized modpack (about 200 mods) centered around the difficult recipes and mechanics of GtceuM. Inspired by modpacks such as GT:NH and Monifactory, ConGregation tries to offer a unique progression, integrating (for now) mods such as Tinker's construct, Create, GtceuM, and more. 

This is an alpha version, so things such as default recipes for mods currently integrated into progression and other bugs are to be expected, please report them if you find any!
## Features
-a rather unique progression going from the very start up until the steam age, where you'll have to search the lands for gravel to turn into flint to be then used into tools. After that, gather resources to make a Tinker's melter setup and a Ex nihilio sieveing setup. After, gather some more gravel to then sieve for metal pieces, which are then melted and casted into ingots. From on there, it's the usual: mining, crafting, building, things you are already familiar with (or is it?)\
-a decently sized quest book, guiding you through your journey\
-a variety of food brought by Farmer's delight, and also a semi-realistic nutrition system\
-a realistic health system, simillar to extraction shooters like Escape from Tarkov, where each limb has it's own health bar\
-quality of life mods such as Iron chests, Iron furnaces and Functional storage\
-exploration mods such as Epic terrain and WWEE which completly overhaul the world around you\
-and many more!
